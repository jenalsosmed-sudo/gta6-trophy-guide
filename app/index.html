Roadmap to the platinum

One playthrough, no online trophies, no difficulty trophies. PowerPyx rates the platinum 4/10 at about 200 hours; Push Square puts it at 300 hours or more. Both are published estimates, not promises — the list below is the order that wastes the least effort.

  1. Phase 1 — Play the story, and save by hand

    Enjoy the campaign at your leisure. Story trophies pop as you finish chapters and missions — the opening mission, the four chapters, the named missions and the finale. Before Chapter 1 ends, do the early meditation spot (see Missables): it is the one trophy reported as possibly missable, and the game has no mission replay. Keep a manual save at the start of every chapter and one before the final mission.

  2. Phase 2 — Stranger missions

    Finish the eight stranger mission strands (Andy, Kenton, Billy, Randy, Tyrone, Bret and the rest). Each finished strand counts toward Strangers & Freaks (silver) and each strand's finale pops its own bronze trophy.

  3. Phase 3 — Activities, while you are out anyway

    Sample all 9 activity types (Master of None), then grind the counted ones: 250 stunts, 42 gold shooting-range medals, 14 drone race wins, 20 different race wins, 50 challenges. Check progress any time at Touch Pad, then Progress.

  4. Phase 4 — Collectibles and the map

    WAiNK scans at 100 locations, 3 collectible card sets (15 for 100%), 18 meditation spots, 100 vehicles discovered, and explore every region of Leonida. Collectible hunts and the exploration trophy do each other's driving.

  5. Phase 5 — Robberies and money

    Five bank vaults, an armored truck, a jewelry store without being spotted, one illegal operation per gang, the partner robberies — then launder $250,000 in Marked Bills, spend $100,000 in shops and buy all 8 garages ($160,000). Register 10 vehicles with Scooter Bros once you have the parking spots.

  6. Phase 6 — 100% completion, then the platinum

    After the story the game unlocks its 100% Completion checklist (Touch Pad, Progress, 100% Completion). Finish what is left of it for A Long Road (gold) — you do not need literally everything, for example only 15 of the 18 card sets count — and Vice Got a Price! pops with the last trophy you earn.

Missable trophies

Built from the official descriptions and the published guides' warnings, as of 19 November 2026. Only one trophy on the whole list is flagged as possibly missable.

Bronze Positive Mental Attitude — possibly missable

Meditate at every spot across Leonida. There are 18 meditation spots. Published guides (Output Lag, reporting PowerPyx) say one early spot — Lucia's balcony in Chapter 1 — disappears after that chapter, and the game has no mission replay, so a missed spot can only be fixed by an earlier save. Do the Chapter 1 meditation before the chapter ends, and keep a manual save at the start of every chapter.

Everything else: safe

Every story trophy unlocks by finishing a chapter or a mission, and after the finale you can keep exploring Leonida in free roam — nothing is locked behind the ending. Collectibles, activities, robberies and money trophies can all be finished after the story. No published guide has flagged any other trophy as missable as of 19 November 2026.

Two warnings that are not missables

  • No mission replay. There is no way to replay a story mission, and no New Game Plus (Output Lag). Manual saves are your only safety net.
  • Cheats switch trophies off. PowerPyx reports that turning on a cheat disables trophies, and GameSpot and PC Gamer add that you cannot save once you have used one (via PlanetGTA6). Cheats only unlock after the story anyway, so finish the platinum hunt first.
