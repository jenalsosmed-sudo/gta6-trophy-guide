Roadmap to Platinum

Play in this order. Story first, then stranger strands, then activities, then collectibles and the world, then crime and money — and 100% completion last, because it sweeps up almost everything else. The whole run is roughly 200 to 300 hours, mostly the clean-up after the story.

Missables

The game has no mission replay, so the safe habit is a manual save at the start of every chapter and one before the final mission. Against that background, only one trophy is flagged as possibly missable:

Why only one?

PowerPyx flags Positive Mental Attitude as possibly missable: one meditation spot, at Jason’s house during Chapter 1, is reported to disappear after that chapter. Push Square’s guide lists no known missables. This guide flags it and tells you how to protect it, rather than pretending the disagreement does not exist.

Everything else on the list can be finished in free roam after the story. The twelve story trophies unlock automatically as you play and cannot be missed.

Sources

    A note on one grade

    Maximum Withdrawal is listed as Silver by Exophase and PSNProfiles, and as Bronze by Push Square’s guide. This guide follows Exophase and PSNProfiles, and says so on the trophy itself.

    What is still unconfirmed

    For seven trophies no published guide had confirmed an exact trigger by 19 November 2026 — Personal Best, Actions Speak Louder, Challenging Tank, In The Beginning…, Capital Offense, Bad Luck, I Guess, The Truth Is Out There and Ten Pin. Their cards say so plainly instead of guessing.
